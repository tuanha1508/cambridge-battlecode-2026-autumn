# Research retained from the contest

The research-first qualifier work aimed at one coherent economy and action
lifecycle. It did not produce a validated replacement before the entry lock.
The qualifier therefore used v792 and finished outside the top eight.

## Evidence gathered

The 3 November global census retained histories for 817 teams. Of these, 800
had eligible current-day matches with both engine references at version 2;
17 did not. Later ladder observations included more teams but no new history
fetches. A global ladder position was never treated as international eligibility
or a tournament result. Private conversations and raw opponent histories stay
outside this portable archive.

The root-bound final research intake queued 259 lost child games, including
losses inside won pairings. Forty were summary-reviewed; 219 remained. There
were 53 distinct deeper match investigations. Those are coverage counts, not
independent experiments or proof of why a particular match was lost.

## Main design work

The candidate policy tried to connect task admission, work already performed,
capture, actual cargo, shopping and delivery into one decision model:

- Admit new economic work only when its final modeled value is finite and
  positive. Keep information gathering on its own contract.
- Record actual successful physical actions before granting an own-work
  continuation. Do not use observed HP decline as evidence of our action.
- Compare finishing, capture and exit using current observations, and retire
  commitments on death, respawn, stale input or changed work.
- Preserve unrelated occupants when forecasting a route through a cleared
  camp. A defeated camp does not erase an enemy or teleport blocker.
- Bank actual carried gems when no positive new task is available.
- Share exact-next-purchase accounting between the live shop and a conditional
  whole-cycle upgrade model. Do not spend a future gem reward twice.

v10 connected these boundaries and passed source and compile audits. Its
objective and access limitations remained, and no game measured its strength.
v11 expanded the resource programme and action integration but retained five
explicit source blockers. See the candidate READMEs for their status.

## Lessons from the result

The final qualifier record contains four strong pairing wins and two losses,
including a 10-11 upper-bracket loss. It does not by itself identify a causal
bot defect. Per-game maps and decisive turns were not fetched in this closeout.
No claim is made that an unplayed candidate would have changed the outcome.

Future work would need validated action semantics, measured fuel, complete
official-engine games, paired comparisons and fresh holdouts. Those checks
were not run as part of this archive task.
